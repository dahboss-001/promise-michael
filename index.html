Bill Angels

A platform connecting people facing essential bills with people ready to help pay them, one bill at a time. Preview build, no real payments enabled.

Case study
What it addresses
People who struggle to pay essential bills such as rent, and people who want to help but need a trustworthy way to do it.
How bills are submitted
Recipients submit the bill amount, category, due date and private supporting documents.
How requests are handled
Requests are meant to be reviewed before they appear in the donor feed. Donors browse requests, contribute to a bill or skip to the next. After a verified payment, the recipient gets a notification and a payment record.
What exists
A public preview landing page that explains the flow, with a clearly labelled sample request. Real payments, verification and the full submission flow are not live yet.
Status
In development.
